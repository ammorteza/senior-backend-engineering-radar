# Contributing

Contributions that improve the Senior Backend Engineering Radar are welcome.

For a proposed blip, include its **name**, **quadrant**, **ring**, why it matters to senior backend engineers, expected depth, and important trade-offs.

Prefer durable concepts and broadly useful technologies that improve architecture, reliability, security, performance, operability or engineering effectiveness. Avoid adding something merely because it is new or fashionable.

Keep `radar.csv` compatible with Thoughtworks BYOR. Allowed quadrants are **Techniques**, **Platforms**, **Tools**, and **Languages & Frameworks**. Allowed rings are **Adopt**, **Trial**, **Assess**, and **Hold**.

A ring change should explain what changed: maturity, production experience, adoption, alternatives, or the expected competency of a senior backend engineer.
